How it works

Numbers appear on the grid. Click 1 and the rest flip face-down. Click the remaining tiles in order from memory. Each round you clear adds a number. Three strikes and you're out.

Why “chimp”

Named after Kyoto University studies where a young chimpanzee, Ayumu, recalled the positions of numerals 1–9 after seeing them for a fraction of a second, outscoring human volunteers.
